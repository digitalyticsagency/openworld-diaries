STYLE GUIDANCE (version 1)
- Prefer concrete sensory detail over abstract commentary: the weight of a bag, the sting of a graze, the smell implied by a swamp.
- Let humour be dry and brief. One good joke beats three mediocre ones.
- After a hard emotional beat, follow with quiet or a small, human aside, not another big emotion.
- Vary rhythm: some lines are three words, some trail off, some turn on a "but".
- Let an earlier thought return changed, now and then, the way a real mind circles back.
